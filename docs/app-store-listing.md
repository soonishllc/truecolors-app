## Description (4,000)

Truecolors shirts look like calm black-and-white line art indoors. Step into the sun and the print fills with full colour in seconds, then fades back when you go inside. Every time.

DESIGN YOUR OWN
Describe an idea or snap a photo of a sketch. Our AI studio draws it as print-ready line art in the style you pick, from detailed scenes to tattoo flash, retro badges and cute mascots, then colours it for the sun. Ask for changes in plain words until it's right. You start with free credits; no card needed.

GET IT MADE, GET PAID
Launch your design as a pre-order with its own page, mockups and a reveal video to share. If 100 people pre-order it, we print it, and you earn on every shirt sold, more through your own link. Nobody pays unless it reaches 100.

BACK THE COMMUNITY
Discover designs from people around the world, watch them change from shade to sun, and pre-order the ones you love. Backers get numbered limited editions, and only pay if the design gets made.

SHOP READY-TO-WEAR
Our own colour-change tees ship today, from Dubai across the UAE and GCC and from Las Vegas across the USA.

KNOW THE MOMENT IT MOVES
Turn on notifications to hear when someone backs your design, how it's doing each day, and when it goes to print.

Printed in Cancún with UV-reactive ink on soft cotton. Machine washable; the effect lasts the life of the shirt.

## App Review information

- **Sign-in:** designers sign in with their Truecolors store account (email code). Create a demo account before submitting, give the reviewer its email and how to receive the code, and give it credits so they can try the studio. (Claude can't create accounts; this one is yours to make.)
- **Notes for the reviewer:**
  > Truecolors sells physical shirts. Checkout is Shopify's, for physical goods only; there are no digital purchases in the app. Design credits are free. To try designing: Create tab → describe an idea → sign in with the demo account → "Draw it". Notifications are offered in the Studio (Me → My studio) and are only sent about the designer's own pre-orders. Accounts can be deleted from Me → Delete my designer account; shop accounts from Me → Delete my shop account.
- **Contact:** the person who answers App Review, with a phone number.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Data **linked to the user**, not used for tracking:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Contact info: name, email | Account, order and pre-order emails |
| Contact info: physical address, phone | Shopify checkout, to ship orders |
| Purchases | Order history, pre-order counts, designer earnings |
| User content: photos (sketches), other content (design ideas, designs) | The design studio |
| Identifiers: user ID | The account |
| Financial info: payout details (designers only) | Paying designers their earnings |

Data **not linked** to the user: usage data (Plausible analytics, which is cookieless and anonymous). No tracking, no third-party advertising, no data sold.
